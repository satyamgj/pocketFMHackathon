# Chapter 170 — "Ashwatthama Is Dead"
## Dialogue-Only Transcript (no narrator)

```
— 0:00 —
SOLDIER (ragged, breathless, close): He does not stop. He does not stop to breathe.

— 1:30 —
ARJUNA (shouting over noise): Fall back to the second line. The second line. Do you hear me?
BHIMA (heavy, breathing hard): Brother. Look at the ground.
ARJUNA (tense): I am looking.
BHIMA (flat, insistent): No. Look at it.

— 3:00 —
SAGE (layered/chorused, unplaceable): Drona.
DRONA (low, flat, unsurprised): I know it.
SAGE (layered/chorused): You were given the celestial weapons on a condition. You know the condition.
SAGE (layered/chorused): They are not to be used against men who cannot answer them. You are using them on farmers. On boys.
DRONA (long pause before this line, quiet): Yes.
SAGE (layered/chorused): This is not war any longer. You have crossed out of it.
SAGE (layered/chorused, gentle but final): Lay down the bow, Drona. You have done what you came to do and more than what you came to do. Lay it down and come with us.

— 6:00 —
YUDHISHTHIRA (dry, quiet): How many.
ARJUNA (strained): We have stopped counting. That is not a figure of speech. The men who were counting are dead.
BHIMA (blunt, flat): Then we go at him together. All of us, at once, and some of us do not come back, and it is finished.
ARJUNA (tense): He has the Brahmastra on the string, Bhima. Together means we die in one place instead of five.
KRISHNA (calm, cutting cleanly through): He cannot be beaten while he is holding that bow.
YUDHISHTHIRA (flat): Then we have no course.
KRISHNA (calm, precise): I said while he is holding it.
ARJUNA (slow, wary): Say what you mean.
KRISHNA (calm, unhurried): Drona has one weakness and he has never hidden it. He has never needed to. Take away his reason to hold the bow and the bow comes down on its own.
ARJUNA (tense): Ashwatthama is on the north flank. He is not even wounded.
KRISHNA (calm, level): Drona does not need him to be dead. Drona needs to believe he is dead.
ARJUNA (flat, hard): No.
KRISHNA (gentle): Arjuna.
ARJUNA (strained, raw): He taught me to hold a bow. He put my hands on it. You are asking me to break an old man by lying to him about his son.
KRISHNA (calm, unbothered): I am telling you what is true. Whether you use it is yours.
BHIMA (flat): There is an elephant in Indravarma's line called Ashwatthama.
BHIMA (flat): The Malava king named it after him. It was meant as flattery.

— 8:30 —
KRISHNA (calm): He will not believe Bhima. He will not believe me. He will not believe any of you.
YUDHISHTHIRA (very quiet): I know who he will believe.
YUDHISHTHIRA (flat, final): No.
KRISHNA (level, unpressured): Then the war ends today, and not in our favour, and everything spent to get here is spent for nothing.
YUDHISHTHIRA (flat): No.
BHIMA (quiet, uncharacteristically so): Twelve thousand this morning. I saw them go.
ARJUNA (barely audible): Brother.
KRISHNA (soft): Bhima.
BHIMA (flat, resigned): I will go.

— 12:00 —
BHIMA (roaring, carrying across a huge distance): ASHWATTHAMA IS DEAD!
BHIMA (roaring, repeating): ASHWATTHAMA IS DEAD! ASHWATTHAMA IS DEAD!

— 13:00 —
DRONA (flat, to himself): Bhima.
DRONA (louder, dismissive): Bhima says it. Bhima would say anything.

— 14:00 —
DRONA (not loud): Yudhishthira.
YUDHISHTHIRA (dry, quiet): Acharya.
DRONA (low): I have taught a great many people. Most of them I taught to shoot.
DRONA (low): I taught you as well, and you were not the best of them, and we both know it. You were something else.
DRONA (low): In your whole life you have never told me an untruth. Not when you were nine and it would have saved you a beating. Not once.
DRONA (low, steady): So I am going to ask you, and then I am going to go back to my line and finish this, one way or the other.
DRONA (quiet, direct): Is my son dead?

— 16:00 —
YUDHISHTHIRA (clear, level, projected — this half should sound designed to carry): Ashwatthama is dead.
YUDHISHTHIRA (quieter, dropped in volume, NOT a whisper — almost swallowed): The elephant.

— 19:00 —
ARJUNA (shouting, urgent): TAKE HIM ALIVE!
ARJUNA (shouting): He is unarmed! He is sitting down! Take him alive, that is an order, take him ALIVE!
ARJUNA (shouting, four repetitions, growing more desperate each time): DHRISHTADYUMNA!

— 20:00 —
ARJUNA (distant, still shouting, desperate): DHRISHTADYUMNA, NO!

— 22:15 —
ASHWATTHAMA (bright, breathless, delighted): Three! That is three!
ASHWATTHAMA (mildly irritated, still upbeat): What is that noise?
ASHWATTHAMA (dismissive, cheerful, already turning away — genuinely no dread in this line): Somebody has won something small and wants everyone to hear about it.
ASHWATTHAMA (cheerful, to himself): Let them. Let them count theirs. I will tell him mine tonight.
```
